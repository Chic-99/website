About CHIC

A local bridge to Chennai's healthcare expertise

Chennai Hospitals Information Centre (CHIC) helps patients in Nellore access visiting Chennai specialists, trusted local doctors, diagnostics, pharmacy, physiotherapy, nursing support and Chennai hospital coordination — all through one local point of contact.

CHIC is not a hospital. It is a coordination centre: reputed specialist doctors from Chennai visit Nellore according to their own schedules, bringing specialist expertise closer to home.
